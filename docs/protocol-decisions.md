# Protocol decisions requiring research-team confirmation

1. Confirm five practice trials are additional to 90 scored items per block (95 presentations per block), rather than included in the 90.
2. Confirm six unique allocations: 90 scored items per block, 45 real words and 45 non-words.
3. Approve the order/interleaving method and whether order is fixed or counterbalanced.
4. Confirm response timeout and whether non-response advances automatically.
5. Approve Kannada instruction script, button labels and practice feedback.
6. Confirm rewards occur after every five completed scored trials regardless of accuracy.
7. Confirm no word is spoken during scored trials and audio is silent during stimulus presentation.
8. Approve native female Kannada voice recordings.
9. Define participant study ID and assessor ID formats.
10. Define whether a child may repeat a visit/block and who authorises it.
11. Approve session interruption, resume and restart rules.
12. Confirm tablet model, Android version, resolution, orientation and browser.
13. Approve local retention, export authorisation, encryption and device loss procedure.
14. Confirm the REDCap import format, event/instance mapping and responsible administrator.
15. Approve item bank versioning, linguistic review and sign-off evidence.

The app must not silently choose answers to these protocol questions. The research PI/statistician must approve the final protocol before field deployment.
